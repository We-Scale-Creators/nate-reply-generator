## V4.7 changes

- Source-winner images may display because they come from Meta.
- Net-new concepts and variations remain text-only in Design and Copy while the visual work lives in Figma.
- Design completion is based on registering the requested Figma variations, not uploading premature final files.
- Finalize & Export shows no current-creative preview until the designer exports the file for Google Drive.
- Exported finals then become visible and flow into Launch.
